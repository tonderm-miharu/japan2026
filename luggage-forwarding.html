How it works宅急便とは

Yamato Transport's 宅急便 (takkyubin) is the standard door-to-door courier. Practically every business hotel in Japan accepts outgoing parcels at reception and holds incoming ones for arriving guests — you rarely need to find a depot yourself.

ItemDetail
Transit timeNext day for most domestic routes. Both of our routes are short-haul and should be next-day — but confirm at the counter, it is not guaranteed.
Size limitSum of width + length + height. Size 140 = 140 cm total / 20 kg. A normal large suitcase is usually size 140–160. Absolute max 200 cm and 30 kg.
CostRoughly ¥2,000–2,800 per suitcase on these distances (size 140–160). Paid in cash by the sender at handover.

Sending a parcel to a hotel where you are not yet staying

This is normal in Japan and hotels expect it, but the waybill must make clear who it belongs to. The convention is to write the hotel as recipient, followed by 気付 (kizuke, "c/o"), then your own name and your check-in date. Both of our shipments arrive before check-in, so both need this. Notify the hotel in advance — see the e-mail template.

Tight window — the one real risk on this trip

You reach Hotel Sambancho on the evening of 7.10. and leave Matsuyama on the morning of 8.10. (day 21, train to Shimonada then the Beppu ferry). The suitcase therefore has a window of roughly one night. If delivery slips by a single day, the bag is in Matsuyama and you are in Kyushu.

Mitigation: specify 配達希望日 = 10月7日 with a morning slot; ask at the counter to confirm next-day is realistic before you pay; save the tracking number and check it on the evening of 6.10. If the counter will not commit to the 7th, do not send — carry the bag or use a coin locker instead.

This one is comfortable, but it arrives two days early

Sent on the 16th, next-day delivery would put the bag at the APA on the 17th — a day before you check in on the 18th. That is fine and hotels routinely hold luggage, but it makes advance notice more important than on shipment 1. Requesting delivery on the 18th is cleaner if the counter allows it.

Also note the tight morning: handover at 07:30 and the Ocean Arrow ferry at 09:25 the same morning. The hotel desk is staffed, but a Yamato depot may not open until 08:00 — settle the evening before which of the two you are using.

At the counterフロントでの手順

Ask the evening before. Confirm the hotel accepts 宅急便, from what time, and whether they hand it to Yamato the same morning. If they do not, ask for the nearest depot (ヤマト運輸の営業所) and its opening time.
Hand over the suitcase and ask for the form. Reception keeps blank waybills (送り状). Use the phrase below.
Fill in the waybill from this sheet. Recipient block first, then sender block, then 品名 and 配達希望日. Staff will help — show them this page.
Pay in cash. Around ¥2,000–2,800. Some hotels add it to the room bill instead.
Keep the tracking slip. You get a tear-off copy with the tracking number (お問い合わせ番号, 12 digits). Photograph it immediately. Track at kuronekoyamato.co.jp.
Check the same evening. Confirm the parcel is moving and the ETA still matches your arrival.

Phrases使えるフレーズ

Sending — at your hotel reception
宅急便でこの荷物を送りたいのですが、お願いできますか。
Takkyubin de kono nimotsu o okuritai no desu ga, onegai dekimasu ka.
I would like to send this luggage by takkyubin — could you help me?
Asking whether next-day delivery is realistic — the important one
明日の午前中に届きますか。確実でしょうか。
Ashita no gozen-chu ni todokimasu ka. Kakujitsu deshou ka.
Will it arrive tomorrow morning? Is that reliable?
Explaining it goes to a hotel you have not checked into yet
宿泊予定のホテルに送ります。チェックインは10月7日です。
Shukuhaku yotei no hoteru ni okurimasu. Chekku-in wa juu-gatsu nanoka desu.
It is going to a hotel where I have a booking. My check-in is on 7 October. (For shipment 2 say 10月18日, juu-gatsu juu-hachi-nichi.)
Receiving — on arrival at the destination hotel
宅急便で荷物が届いているはずですが、確認していただけますか。
Takkyubin de nimotsu ga todoite iru hazu desu ga, kakunin shite itadakemasu ka.
A parcel should have arrived for me by takkyubin — could you check?
If something has gone wrong
荷物がまだ届いていません。お問い合わせ番号はこちらです。
Nimotsu ga mada todoite imasen. O-toiawase bangou wa kochira desu.
My parcel has not arrived yet. Here is the tracking number.

Send this a few days before each shipment — both parcels arrive before you check in, so the hotel should know to expect and hold them. Replace the bracketed parts.
